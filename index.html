Bloomwood Seasons

A cozy merge garden that follows the real seasons.

Merge flowers and tools, bring an old garden back to life, and watch it change with the season wherever you call home. There are ten regional garden styles, from Mediterranean to Nordic to rainforest. You can play offline, and you don't need an account.
