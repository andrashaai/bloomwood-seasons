Support & FAQ

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Something not quite right, or just curious? Here are answers to the most common questions. If yours isn't here, contact us.

Playing

How do I play?

Tap a generator (like the Seed Pouch) to make an item. Each tap usually uses 1 ⚡ energy. Put two identical items together to merge them into the next, better item. You can drag one onto the other, or tap one and then tap the other.

Visitors ask you for items in their orders. Deliver them to earn 🪙 coins, experience and ⭐ stars, then spend stars in the Garden to restore it step by step. Restoring areas unlocks new generators, visitors and places.

My board is full. What can I do?

Merge what you can first. You can also move items into storage, or sell items you don't need for coins. You can buy more storage slots with coins or gems.

My energy ran out. What now?

Energy refills by itself, 1 point every 90 seconds up to 100, even while the game is closed. You also get energy when you level up and from the daily reward. If you're in a hurry, you can refill it in the Shop with gems, or watch an optional short ad for +50 energy (up to 3 times a day).

Seasons and places

How do the seasons work?

Your garden follows the real-world season of the home location you choose. Depending on the garden style, a year has four seasons, a wet and a dry season, or a hot and a cool season. There are ten regional garden styles. Each season brings its own flower chain that you can merge and collect in the Herbarium, and the Season Box makes items from the current season. Items from past seasons stay on your board.

How do I change my home location?

Open Settings and tap your home. Your first change is free. After that you can change it once every 30 days, and the picker shows the date of your next change. You pick the home from a list, and the game never uses your real location or GPS.

Can I see gardens in other places?

Yes. Open Garden and tap Visit to look at any garden style in any season. Visiting never changes your own garden, and you can send a postcard from there.

Saving and devices

How is my progress saved?

The game saves automatically on your device, so there's nothing to press and no account to create. Everything works offline.

Can I move my progress to another device, or play on two devices?

Not directly. Progress is stored only on the device you play on, and it isn't synced through iCloud between devices, so an iPhone and an iPad each have their own garden. When you set up a new device by restoring a full backup of your old one (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), your garden comes along with the backup. We don't keep a copy of your progress, so we can't move or recover it for you.

How do I start over or delete my data?

Delete the app (press and hold its icon → Remove App → Delete App) and install it again from the App Store. This erases your garden and all game data on that device. Gems and items you have bought are erased too and can't be restored. Remove Ads can be restored afterwards (see below).

Purchases

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, or use the Restore Purchases button at the bottom of the Shop. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. Restoring brings back Remove Ads. Gem packs and the Starter Pack are used up as you play, so they can't be restored.

I paid but didn't get my item.

Close and reopen the game while connected to the internet. The game picks up any finished purchase when it starts. If you used Ask to Buy, the item arrives after the purchase is approved. If it still hasn't arrived, try Restore purchases, then contact us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds go through Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds ourselves.

What does Remove Ads do?

It turns off the occasional ad breaks for good. Optional ads that you choose to watch for a reward (like extra energy) are still available if you want them.

How can I stop my child from making purchases?

Use Apple's Screen Time (Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases) to block in-app purchases, or Ask to Buy with Family Sharing so each purchase needs your approval.

Comfort and accessibility

What accessibility options are there?
  • VoiceOver: every board cell is read out separately with the item it holds, and buttons have spoken labels.
  • Tap to merge: you never have to drag. Tap an item, then tap a matching one.
  • Reduce motion: switch it on in the game's Settings. The game also follows the iOS Reduce Motion setting.
  • Sound and haptics: music, sound effects and vibration can each be turned off in Settings.
  • Larger text: menus and pop-ups grow with your iOS text size setting (the board itself keeps a fixed size so it fits the screen).

Something hard to use? Please tell us. We want everyone to be able to enjoy the garden.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Everything can be played offline. You only need a connection to make or restore purchases.

What about my privacy?

No account, no tracking and no personal data collected by us. Your garden stays on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Still need help?

Contact us on GitHub

Open a support request on GitHub (you'll need a free GitHub account). It helps if you tell us:

  • your device model and iOS version (Settings → General → About);
  • the game version (at the bottom of the game's Settings);
  • what happened and what you expected, plus a screenshot if you can.

Requests on GitHub are public. Please don't include your email address, purchase receipts or other personal details. For a private matter, such as a privacy request, just say so, and we'll arrange a private way to continue. We usually reply within a few days.
